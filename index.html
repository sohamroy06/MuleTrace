Data Sources & Limits

This prototype uses only passive, publicly available intelligence sources:

• RDAP/WHOIS: Public domain registration data via standard RDAP protocol

• DNS: Public DNS record lookups (A, AAAA, MX, NS, TXT, CNAME)

• Certificate Transparency: Public CT log entries for SSL/TLS certificates

• IP Reputation: Public threat intelligence feeds and blocklists

No active scanning or probing of third-party servers is performed. No access to real bank systems. No freezing of accounts. All subscriber, login, KYC and access records are obtainable only through the applicable lawful process.

All geolocation and hosting attribution data is labelled "Indicative" — it does not constitute proof of identity or location.
